Hand weaving in South London

Roz Cottrell is a textile designer working with an Electronic Loom in South London. She studied weave at the Royal College of Art and has experience as a freelance collaborator with architects and furniture designers, as well as prior work as a weave technician at Chelsea College of Art.

The current collection consists of bespoke cushions created using a plain warp and extra weft patterning. The work uses simple shapes and a limited colour palette, using the warp like a page in a sketch book.

Each cushion features:

  • One-piece weave construction
  • Extra weft pattern on the front; plain colour on the back
  • Individual colour-matched zips
  • Duck feather filling
